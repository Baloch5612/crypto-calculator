How to Calculate Crypto Profits and Losses

Calculating cryptocurrency capital gains requires assessing the difference between your acquisition price and your liquidation price while factoring in transaction costs.

The Math Formula

Total Coins Purchased = Investment Amount / Buy Price

Gross Returns = Total Coins Purchased * Sell Price

Net Gains/Losses = Gross Returns - Investment Amount - Total Fees

Return on Investment (ROI) = (Net Gains / Investment Amount) * 100
