How This Converter Works

This tool pulls live market prices and lets you convert between major cryptocurrencies and fiat currencies instantly. Pick what you're converting from and to, enter an amount, and the result updates automatically using current exchange rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often do the rates update?

Rates are fetched live each time you load the page or press Refresh Rates. Crypto prices can move quickly, so always refresh before relying on a number for a real transaction.

Can I convert between two cryptocurrencies?

Yes. Choose any two assets from the dropdowns, including two different coins — the converter calculates the exchange rate between them automatically using their current USD prices.

Is this the same rate I'll get on an exchange?

This shows the underlying market price, not a specific exchange's buy or sell price. Actual trades usually include a spread and trading fees, so the amount you receive on an exchange will be slightly different.
